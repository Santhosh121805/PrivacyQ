Training mental-health models across institutions, without the data ever leaving.

What it does

Data stays put

Each school trains on its own records. Only model updates ever leave.

Privacy you can measure

Formal differential privacy with a stated budget, not a promise.

An honest test of quantum

Quantum and classical models compared on identical data.

What we found

How it works

01
De-identify

Identifiers removed before anything else.

02
Preprocess

Imputation and scaling fitted on training rows only.

03
Encode to qubits

4–8 features loaded into a quantum circuit.

04
Train privately

DP-SGD at each site, combined by federated averaging.

05
Evaluate

Cross-validation, then a hold-out scored once.
