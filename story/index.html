The problem

Three schools each hold student wellbeing survey data. None of them is allowed to share it.

Three schools. Three weak models.
No way to combine them.

Training together

Each school trains on its own data. Only model updates travel to the server — never student records.

The result

The same privacy protection (ε = 4) applies to the schools alone and together.

Every school now has a model none of them could have built alone with the same privacy protection, and no student record ever left the building.

Did quantum help?

Both models trained on the same 1,500 student records with the same 8 features, tested 15 times.

We tested whether a quantum model does better on this data. It does not. Knowing that is the result.

Real data: NSCH 2024 public survey, ages 12–17. Every number is from a recorded experiment.
